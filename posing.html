Mandatory pose practice
How it works: pick a pose, hold it steady for ~1.5s and the camera auto-captures a still to compare against the reference photo.

Judge's-eye readiness check-in (monthly)
When: Right after your daily mobility session, while ROM is at its maximum (within 30 min of active stretching). For the right lat spread specifically: pose right after overhead lat stretch and T-spine work, while that tissue is open.
Tip: Film it weekly — you will notice a difference.
Mandatory Poses
1. Front Double Biceps
Facing forward, both arms raised and flexed, fists near shoulder height, legs braced.
Brace legs and flex quads, don't just flex arms — judges score the whole physique.
2. Front Lat Spread
Hands on waist, elbows driven wide and forward to spread the lats, chest up.
Right-side lat spread is limited by the shoulder — practice this one right after overhead lat stretch.
3. Side Chest
Turn to your best side, front leg bent and flexed, grip opposite wrist in front of chest, crush the pecs.
4. Side Triceps
Turn to your side, near arm extended straight down and back to show triceps horseshoe, far hand grips the wrist.
5. Back Double Biceps
Back to the mirror, same arm flex as front double biceps, stagger the legs to show calves.
6. Back Lat Spread
Back to the mirror, hands on waist, drive elbows wide to show full back width.
7. Abdominal & Thigh
Hands behind head, crunch forward slightly, one leg extended and flexed to show quad separation.
Posing Tip
Do your mobility session first, then practice posing immediately after. ROM is at its maximum within 30 min of active stretching. For the right lat spread: practice after overhead lat stretch and T-spine work while the tissue is open. Film it weekly — you will notice a difference.
Use: Content/photoshoot poses, not competition-judged poses. Pick a few that fit the shot, don't run through all 14 every time.
Content Poses
Archer Pose
Step one leg out wide. Turn torso and extend front arm straight. Pull rear arm back as if drawing a bow. Chest open and lifted. Look over front arm.
Focus: chest, arms, lats, obliques, V-taper. Best angle: 3/4. Best lighting: side light to rear or 45° front.
Icarus / Winged Kneeling
Kneel on one knee with the other leg extended out. Open arms wide and slightly back. Spread chest and lift chin. Look to the side or slightly up.
Focus: lats, chest, shoulders, abs, overall aesthetic. Best angle: slightly low. Best lighting: top or side for dramatic shadows.
Arnold Classic (Double Biceps)
Stand tall, feet shoulder width. Raise both arms and flex biceps. Elbows slightly forward. Chest up, abs tight. Look to the side.
Focus: biceps, shoulders, chest, V-taper, overall width. Best angle: straight on. Best lighting: top/front to bring out detail.
Classical Statue Pose (Frank Zane Style)
Stand tall. Raise one arm high with hand open. Flex opposite arm at side. Slightly turn torso and look up. Point fingers for aesthetic effect.
Focus: aesthetics, flow, chest, abs, arms, overall symmetry. Best angle: 3/4. Best lighting: top spotlight or soft overhead.
Classic Statue Pose (Bodybuilding Art Pose)
Shift weight to one leg. Raise one arm with hand open. Other arm extended or flexed. Twist torso slightly and look up. Point toes and engage legs.
Focus: aesthetics, flow, full body balance, classic physique. Best angle: 3/4. Best lighting: top spotlight for sculpted look.
Flexed Side Triceps
Turn to the side. Step one leg back for balance. Flex triceps by keeping arm straight and pulling back. Keep chest up and abs tight. Look forward or slightly down.
Focus: triceps, shoulders, chest, obliques. Best angle: side. Best lighting: side lighting to highlight triceps.
Twisting Rear Biceps
Turn back toward camera at 3/4 angle. Look over shoulder. Flex rear bicep and forearm. Squeeze back and rear delt. Keep waist tight.
Focus: back, rear delts, biceps, obliques, detail & thickness. Best angle: 3/4 back. Best lighting: side lighting for separation.
Low Hero Pose
Stand with one foot forward and knee slightly bent. Flex arms (any style) and puff chest. Lean slightly forward for dominance. Keep jaw tight and look up/forward.
Focus: chest, arms, shoulders, presence, overall impact. Best angle: low. Best lighting: top/back for hero effect.
Cross-Body Side Chest
Turn sideways. Bring front arm across body and grip wrist. Squeeze chest and bicep. Pull shoulder back and up. Tighten abs and legs.
Focus: chest, biceps, shoulders, obliques, legs. Best angle: side. Best lighting: side lighting to add depth.
Greek God Hands-on-Hips
Place hands on hips. Elbows flared out. Chest up and wide. Abs tight. Look up and to the side.
Focus: V-taper, chest, abs, shoulders, overall aesthetics. Best angle: 3/4. Best lighting: top/front for classic look.
One-Knee Classical Pose
Kneel on one knee, front foot flat, other knee down. One arm behind head, other arm flexed or resting on leg. Twist torso slightly and look up. Keep abs tight.
Focus: abs, obliques, chest, arms, legs, flow. Best angle: 3/4. Best lighting: top/side for depth.
Arms Overhead Abdominal Stretch
Interlock fingers and extend arms overhead. Stretch up and slightly lean to one side. Keep abs tight and ribcage down. Look down or to the side.
Focus: abs, serratus, obliques, length, lines. Best angle: 3/4. Best lighting: soft top light to show abs.
Arms-Down Lat Flare
Stand tall. Keep arms straight and slightly away from body. Flare lats by pushing elbows forward slightly. Chest up, abs tight. Fists clenched.
Focus: lats, shoulders, chest, V-taper, overall width. Best angle: straight on. Best lighting: top or front to enhance width.
Victory / Freedom Pose
Stand tall and open. Raise one arm up in victory. Other arm flexed at side. Chest lifted. Look up toward hand.
Focus: arms, chest, abs, lats, presence. Best angle: low. Best lighting: top or back for silhouette effect.
General Photo Tips
Lighting: Natural light or strong side light works best.
Angles: Lower angles = more power. 3/4 angles = more depth.
Background: Clean, simple backgrounds keep the focus on you.
Pump: Get a good pump before shooting for maximum detail.
Confidence: Relax, breathe, and own every shot.
