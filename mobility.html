Mobility

Joint care · warm-up · daily protocol
When: Morning, or any time you have 10 min. Best right after waking up — joints are stiff from sleep and this resets everything fast.
Goal: Undo bodybuilder postural damage. Open chest, hip flexors, T-spine, hips, hamstrings.
Daily Bodybuilder Reset (10 min)
1. Dead Hang
45 sec — Hang from a pull-up bar with straight arms. Relax shoulders. Let body hang. Breathe and hold.
Cues: shoulders down, don't shrug, full hang, breathe deep. Best for: shoulders, lats, spine.
2. Thoracic Foam Roller Extension
8 reps — Place roller across mid back. Support head with hands. Extend over roller. Hold and breathe.
Cues: support head, extend upper back, don't extend lower back, breathe and relax. Best for: T-spine, posture, chest.
3. World's Greatest Stretch
5 reps/side — Step one foot forward into a deep lunge. Place opposite hand inside front foot. Rotate torso toward front leg and reach opposite arm up. Hold and return.
Cues: keep back leg straight, rotate through upper back, drive front knee out, breathe deep. Best for: hips, T-spine, groin.
4. Couch Stretch
30 sec/side — Kneel with one knee on the ground. Place back foot on an elevated surface. Squeeze glute and push hips forward. Hold and switch.
Cues: posterior pelvic tilt, squeeze glute, keep torso tall, don't overarch low back. Best for: hip flexors, quads, core.
5. 90/90 Hip Switches
10 reps — Sit with both knees bent at 90 degrees. Rotate both legs to one side. Switch sides. Move slowly and controlled.
Cues: keep chest tall, rotate from hips, stay tall (don't lean), use hands for support. Best for: hips, groin, low back.
6. Deep Squat Hold
30 sec — Squat as deep as possible. Keep chest up. Elbows inside knees. Hold position.
Cues: chest tall, push knees out, stay deep, breathe deep. Best for: hips, ankles, groin.
7. Cat-Cow
10 reps — Start on hands and knees. Arch back, look up (cow). Round back, tuck chin (cat). Repeat slowly.
Cues: move through the spine, breathe with the movement, don't force ROM, smooth and controlled. Best for: spine, core, warm-up.
8. Child's Pose Rotation
8 reps/side — Sit back in child's pose. Walk hands to one side. Rotate chest up. Reach arm up. Hold and switch.
Cues: keep hips back, rotate through upper back, reach arm up, breathe and relax. Best for: T-spine, shoulders, lats.
9. Vacuum Practice
5 breaths — Exhale fully, draw the abdomen inward and up, hold gently, breathe shallow through the chest, then release.
Skill practice, not a stretch — closes out the daily reset and doubles as posing prep.
Minimum Viable (3 Min)
1. T-spine roller — 60 sec
2. World's greatest stretch — 3 reps/side
3. Couch stretch — 45 sec/side
Better than nothing by a significant margin.
Before: Dynamic only. Static >60 sec pre-lift measurably reduces force output — move through ranges, don't hold.
After: Static holds only. Tissue is warm — hold 45–60 sec minimum. Anything less doesn't create lasting change.
Right shoulder — torn posterior labrum. Right-side rotation significantly limited. Knees and elbows chronically aching. Posing (lat spread) affected by right-side restriction.
Every Day — 5–8 Min (Training or Not)
1. Cross-body shoulder stretch
3 × 30 sec, RIGHT side emphasis — Pull arm across chest with opposite hand, shoulder blade pinned against wall. Hold longer on the right.
Targets the posterior capsule — the main restriction causing your limited rotation. Do not skip this one.
2. Banded external rotation isometric
3 × 20–30 sec/arm — Light-moderate band, elbow tucked at side. Activates rotator cuff and scapular stabilizers — the muscles compensating for the torn labrum.
3. T-spine extension over foam roller
2–3 × 10 slow reps — Roller at T5–T8. Arms crossed or hands behind head. Drop head back and breathe into the extension. Work up and down the thoracic spine.
Critical. You cannot fix the shoulder in isolation if the T-spine is frozen. This is what unlocks rotation.
4. Dead hang
2 × 20–30 sec — Hang from a bar, fully relaxed, feet off ground. Decompresses the GH joint, gently stretches the posterior capsule.
If shoulder is flaring: supported hang with feet lightly touching the ground.
Right Shoulder — Posterior Labrum Recovery
Side-lying ER with light DB
3 × 15 · 2–5 lbs — Directly loads infraspinatus and teres minor — the muscles stabilizing the GH joint when the labrum is compromised.
Wall slides with upward rotation
2 × 10 — Retrains serratus anterior and lower trap. Without these firing, every arm elevation creates grinding and impingement.
90/90 shoulder rotation drill
2 × 10 reps — Arm at 90° abduction, rotate palm down then up slowly. Lubricates the GH joint through its actual range.
Avoid: Upright rows · Behind-neck press · Behind-neck pulldown · Sleeping on the right shoulder · Arm behind body in internal rotation (stretches posterior labrum directly).

Meaningful change takes 8–12 weeks of daily consistency. No shortcuts.
Elbow Care — Tendinopathy Protocol
Wrist isometrics (flexor + extensor)
5 × 15 sec each — Flexor: palm up, push into resistance. Extensor: palm down, push back of hand up. Do before working sets that bother you.
Isometrics reduce tendon pain for 45+ min acutely (Cook & Purdam, 2009).
Tyler Twist (Theraband Flexbar)
3 × 15 daily — Grip bar at both ends, one hand pronated one supinated. Flex both wrists, then slowly let the painful side extend against resistance.
Gold standard for lateral epicondylitis. Takes 6–8 weeks for full effect. Bisset et al. (2006) — superior to corticosteroid injections at 1 year.
Reverse wrist curls (eccentric)
3 × 15 · 3× per week on arm days — Light DB (5–10 lbs), palm down. Lower wrist slowly 3–4 sec down, 1 sec up.
Knee Care — Patellar Tendinopathy Protocol
Spanish squat isometric
5 × 45 sec before leg sessions — Knee bent 60–90°. Isometrics reduce patellar tendon pain acutely — do them, then train.
Reverse Nordic curl
3 × 5, build to 3 × 15 over 6–8 weeks — Kneel on pad, feet anchored under rack. Lower torso backward slowly (3–4 sec) as far as pain allows, pull back up.
Gold standard eccentric for patellar tendinopathy. Actually remodels the tendon (Purdam et al. 2004, Visnes & Bahr 2007).
TKE with band
3 × 15/leg — Band looped behind knee, slight knee bend, extend fully. Fixes patellar tracking by strengthening VMO.
Foam roller — outer quad + IT band
60 sec/leg — Find tight spots and hold 10–15 sec. Also roll quad muscle belly and calves (tight calves pull on posterior knee chain).
After leg day: Elevate legs 10–15 min · Ice directly on patellar tendon 10 min if acutely painful.
Flare-Up Rules
Do not go to zero. Total rest causes regression — connective tissue needs load signals to maintain integrity.
Shoulder
Drop to cables/machines. Keep banded ER isometrics. Skip heavy pressing for the session.
Elbow
Drop to bodyweight curls or isometrics. Use straps for back. Keep the Tyler Twist — it helps during flares.
Knee
Spanish squat holds + TKE only. No loaded squatting until 2–3 days clear.
Stop immediately if: catching, popping, or a feeling of joint shifting/giving way. That is instability — not soreness. Back off the movement for the full week.
